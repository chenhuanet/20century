Painting record · 1902–1904

Mont Sainte-Victoire

欧洲 · 油画（布面）

Mont Sainte-Victoire was a motif Cézanne returned to again and again in his late years. The mountain is broken into parallel strokes — ochre, grey-green, pale blue — like masonry and like breathing; trees and fields in the foreground have no clear edges, only blocks of colour advancing and receding. Sky and mountain share one scale of tones; space is built not by perspective but by temperature of colour and direction of stroke.

Cézanne painted the same view dozens of times, not to record landscape but to find structure. He moved painting from "what is seen" to "how seeing is organised," a step that Cubism would take up.
